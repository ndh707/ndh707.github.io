## About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University. I design and study interactive systems that augment human cognition, with a focus on how AI and extended reality can help people direct attention and make sense of information. I am interested in designing assistance around people’s goals and cognitive needs while supporting their agency and judgment.

My current work explores diminished reality for attention support and AI-supported scholarly reading. Much of my research so far focuses on cognitive accessibility for people with ADHD. I combine co-design, interactive prototypes, and user studies to understand people’s existing practices and explore new forms of support.

At SFU, I work with Lawrence Kim, Wolfgang Stuerzlinger, and Parmit Chilana. I am currently visiting KAIST as an exchange student and research intern at KIXLAB, working with Juho Kim.
